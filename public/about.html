About the Teacher 🍎

Meet Mrs. Hobson, your Room 21 teacher.

Heather Hobson

I am so excited to welcome you to TK in Room 21 at Fuerte! This is my thirteenth year at Fuerte. I have taught 16 years in kindergarten, one year as a TK/K combo, and this is my third year in a straight TK group. I have also taught preschool, first grade, third grade, fifth grade, and reading intervention — altogether, this is my 27th year of teaching.

I grew up in Spokane, Washington and attended Washington State University — Go Cougs! There I received a BA in Early Childhood Education and a BA in Elementary Education. I moved to San Diego in 1999 with my husband and earned my MA in Reading Curriculum in Elementary Education from SDSU.

I have three boys who are all former Fuerte Falcons. Rilen is now a senior at Valhalla, and Braedy and Cam are attending college locally. I am so proud to be a Fuerte Falcon! I'm looking forward to a great year!!
